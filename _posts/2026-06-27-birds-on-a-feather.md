I took my camera to "The Monastery" today, which is a park in Cumberland, RI.  If you go down "Nine Men's Misery" trail, you will come upon a couple of ponds where a lot of birds like to congregate.  The birds can often be seen perching among the feathery reeds, making for some intriguing photos.

Here are some Red-winged Blackbirds.
