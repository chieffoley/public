Our stay at the Homewood Suites was now over and we were moving to an air-bnb in Lucas' neck of the woods.  Since we had some time to kill in between check-out and check-in, we decided to visit Silver Lake in the Wasatch Mountains up near Brighton.

The lake is gorgeous, you will feel like you are in the Swiss Alps here.

The road to get there goes along the valley floor between a series of mountains.  With the bright fall colors it makes for a very scenic drive.  We took the roof off the Jeep to get the full effect.
