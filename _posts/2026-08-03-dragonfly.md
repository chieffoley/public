Dragonflies are great to have around, as they eat mosquitoes.  They are actually fine-tuned mosquito-hunting machines.  In addition, they are a marvel to look at, filled with such intricate detail.  It had recently rained, and the wings on this dragonfly have just a couple of tiny drops of water still on it.  I find the structure of the delicate, many-faceted wings spellbinding.
