I woke up early this morning, still on east coast time, and headed out to Tunnel Springs Park, which is just north of the city.  The elevation up there is about 5200 feet at the trail head.  The internet told me it was a good place to see the sun rise.  My plan was to hike just a tiny bit on the Bonneville Shoreline Trail.  There is no shore there, but they say millions of years ago this used to be lake front property.

<a href='javascript:void(0);' name='map-tunnel-springs-park'></a>

As soon as I go onto the trail, I was greeted by 2 plump little porcupines ambling up the path.  They were just as chill as could be, not worried about a thing in the world with all those quills protecting them.  It was still dark out, so I did the best I could to get a picture of them.

After that I wandered onto the trail and started to look for a good place to set up for sunrise pictures, having really no idea what to expect.  Off to the west you can see the airport and some distant mountains.  To the northwest is The Great Salt Lake, but that is not yet visible; I will need to get higher to see that.

There were plenty of hills to the east, so I turned and started to make my way up those hills, trying to get a better vantage point.  It looked simple enough, I would just get up to the crest of that hill and ...

<a href='javascript:void(0);' name='pic-0004'></a>
![0004](/images/2026-09-23-tunnel-springs-sunrise/utah-2026-09-23-morning-tunnel-springs-0004.jpg)
_&nbsp; <a href='{% link photo_info/pi-2026-09-23-utah-2026-09-23-morning-tunnel-springs-0004.md %}'><i class='fa fa-info-circle' style='font-size: 0.73em;'></i></a>_

I guess I'm not used to the altitude here, because as soon as I started climbing up all the breath left me and after a few minutes I was gasping for air.  Excited to see what I could see from up there, I was going too fast, so I had to intentionally slow down my pace, and take breaks.  I crested the hill and at the top I saw that the path continued and there was another hill crest even higher.  Well, if the view was good from here, it must be even better up there, so up I went, again.  Wouldn't you know it, when I got to the top of that I found that it was hiding yet another peak a little further up, so up I went.  Then, when I crested that peak, I found one more hiding behind that, but by this time the darkness had faded and the sun was getting ready to rise and I had a feeling that there might be no end to this nesting doll set of peaks, they could go on one behind the other forever like this.  So, I stopped climbing here.  I was now at about 5800 feet.  Here is the map of my hike:
