Kristin and I went "glamping" at Kissimmee Prairie Preserve State Park in Okeechobee Florida.  This is supposed to be the darkest place in Florida, good for star gazing and such.  The park also has nature trails, bird watching and other things to do.

We were staying in a big canvas tent, complete with microwave, refrigerator, and air conditioning - so, not exactly roughing it.  The map below shows the campground, with a red arrow pointing to our tent.

After it got dark we headed to the Astronomy Pad.  I had pictures in my head of dozens of scientists feverishly working to find alien life or something like that.  Needless to say, I was sorely disappointed.  The "Astronomy Pad" is a grassy area with a few parking spots.  There were a few normal looking people doing not much at all.  I actually got better pictures on the walk to the pad than at the pad itself.

It is dark in this park, but not nearly as dark as you might think.  You cannot see the Milky Way as was advertised.  I have been to places in Maine that are a lot darker and where you can actually see the Milky Way quite clearly.
